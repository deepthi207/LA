Hi Deepthi,

 

My name is Ami and I’m the Founder and Executive Director of Idealist. We launched idealist.org in 1996 on one computer in somebody’s apartment, dreaming of better ways to close the gap between intention and action.

From there, we built a site that’s become the place to find nonprofit jobs, internships, and volunteer opportunities, with resources for anyone who wants to work or volunteer in the social-impact sector. Over the years, we’ve brought together thousands of organizations and millions of people who want to make an impact.

But our dream has always been broader than that: for idealist.org to be the place that people go to take action of any kind, whether finding work with purpose or connecting with neighbors around a shared problem.

 

Below, and over the next few days, our team will share some resources to help you navigate our site and build a social-impact career you love. I hope you’ll also keep our broader vision in mind—we’d love for you to join us.

Ami Dar

Founder and Executive Director
